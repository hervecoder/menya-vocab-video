# Menya Icyongereza — video-ready pilot batches

- **10 word videos** × 7 pairs (14 lines each)
- **6 sentence videos** × 4 pairs (8 lines each)
- Remainder: **4 words** and **2 sentence pairs** (not complete batches)
- Direction: English → Kinyarwanda

Only complete batches are listed as videos. The remainder is kept separately so no entry is lost.
